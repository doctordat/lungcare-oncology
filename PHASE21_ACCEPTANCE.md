# Phase 21 — Product Reframe + Modern Patient Journey

- Product is positioned as patient tracking + clinical decision support, not an EHR.
- Patient can complete a pre-visit questionnaire for new visit, follow-up, or early review.
- Patient can report symptoms with structured severity fields.
- Patient can attach a small photo or voice note to a symptom check-in in the local demo.
- Patient can track medication adherence and common toxicity signals.
- Severe symptom/toxicity signals create an unread team alert in the demo.
- Doctor and Nurse workspaces surface patient-reported pre-visit, symptom, toxicity, and media signals near the top of the active case.
- Patient-reported data is explicitly marked as requiring clinician verification before treatment decisions.
- Current versioned care-plan release flow remains separate from patient self-reporting.
- UI is calmer, more scan-friendly, responsive, and patient workspace is mobile-first.
- Media remains local-browser demo data; no backend upload, cross-device sync, EHR write, autonomous diagnosis, or prescription.
