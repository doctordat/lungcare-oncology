# Phase 23 — Urgent Toxicity Call & Shared Triage

- Patient workspace exposes one prominent urgent support action during treatment follow-up.
- Patient urgent request records the reason, note, current timestamp and increments team unread alerts.
- Doctor and Nurse receive the same open urgent triage session for the active patient.
- Clinician triage popup surfaces current treatment, patient medication/toxicity status and latest symptom context before questions are asked.
- During the call, clinician can capture red flags, urgency level, note and agreed disposition.
- Completing triage appends a clinical event and closes the active triage session without deleting history.
- Patient is explicitly warned not to wait for app response when symptoms may be life-threatening.
- The demo does not claim a real call-center backend, real-time cross-device signaling or automatic outbound calling.
- No autonomous diagnosis, medication change, prescription, dose selection or treatment order is performed by the app.
