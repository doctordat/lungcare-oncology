# Phase 9 acceptance

- Recommendation cards render only when Phase 8 routing data is complete.
- Stage IV + confirmed driver shows driver-directed systemic therapy and alternate/trial strategy classes.
- Stage IV + no actionable driver shows immunotherapy-based, chemo-immunotherapy, and cytotoxic fallback classes.
- Resectable stage III shows perioperative multimodality/surgery and postoperative risk-adapted classes.
- Unresectable stage III shows definitive chemoradiation, consolidation, and sequential modality classes.
- Every card contains: why this option, conditions to verify, watchouts/limitations, and guideline provenance.
- Cards never contain drug doses, prescription instructions, autonomous orders, or a claim that one card is automatically preferred for the individual patient.
- If routing has blockers, recommendation cards are hidden and blockers remain visible.

Evidence basis:
- ASCO Stage IV NSCLC Living Guidelines v2026.3.3, updated July 24, 2026, with and without driver alterations.
- ASCO Management of Stage III NSCLC guideline and rapid recommendation updates, including July 23, 2024 update.
